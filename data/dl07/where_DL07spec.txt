Reference emission spectra of Draine & Li (2007), ApJ 657, 810.

Files: U1.00_1.00_{MW3.1_00..MW3.1_60,LMC2_00,LMC2_05,LMC2_10,smc}.txt
       (U = 1, single starlight intensity, 11 published dust models)

Distributed by B. T. Draine at
  https://www.astro.princeton.edu/~draine/dust/irem.html
as the DL07spec archive, whose README.txt describes the models and the
file format (the spectra start after 61 header lines; column 1 is
lambda in um, column 2 is nu*dP/dnu per H in erg s^-1 H^-1).

Copies of the DL07spec/U1.00/ files that were unpacked at the root of
this working tree. They are read directly by the figure scripts of the
JKAS paper; the code itself does not use them.
